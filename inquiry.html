Rules & add-ons

Built to keep the process smooth.

Travel: Dallas, Denton, and UNT are standard areas. Additional travel may require a fee.

Deposit: A non-refundable deposit secures the date once the shoot is approved.

Rush delivery: Faster turnaround may be available for an added fee.

Extra edits: Additional final edits can be added after the gallery is delivered.

Rescheduling: Weather and emergencies happen. Last-minute changes may require a new deposit.

Late arrival: Sessions begin at the booked time. Late arrivals may reduce shoot time.
